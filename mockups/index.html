Design mockups

All three use the same sample build. Each one is interactive: switch levels, try the level-up (pick, then tap deck cards to drop), and open a card. Click a title to open it full screen.

A · Glacier

Dark, icy and image-first. App-style bottom tabs, level pills, sticky 12-card strip during level-up.

B · Field Notes

Light, editorial, list-first. Sidebar level timeline, guidance inline, motivations as a journal, 3-step level-up wizard.

C · Tabletop

Board-game feel. Campaign track for levels 1–9, fanned hand, "offering" level-up, Gantt-style chronicle overview.
